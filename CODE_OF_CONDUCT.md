# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, useful community for contributors and users of every background and experience level.

Be considerate, use welcoming language, give constructive technical feedback, respect differing viewpoints, and accept responsibility for mistakes. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Protect others' privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized content directed at participants, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where you represent this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, warn participants, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation, depending on the behavior and impact. Enforcement should be proportional and consider context. Maintainers are also subject to this policy.

For conduct concerns, contact a repository maintainer through a private contact method they publish on their GitHub profile. If none is available, request a private contact route without posting identifying or sensitive details publicly. Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern in good faith.
